# Banking Customer Churn Analytics

### SQL | Power BI | Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes banking customer data to identify churn patterns, understand customer behavior, and provide actionable insights to support customer retention strategies.

The analysis covers **10,000 banking customers** across France, Germany, and Spain.

## Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL:** Data exploration and SQL analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and business metrics
- **CSV:** Dataset storage and data import

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | 10,000 |
| Churned Customers | 2,038 |
| Retained Customers | 7,962 |

| Retention Rate | 79.62% |

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Overview:** Overall customer churn performance and key metrics.
2. **Customer Segmentation:** Customer demographics, credit scores, and account balances.
3. **Churn Analysis:** Churn patterns by age, gender, geography, tenure, and banking products.
4. **Product Analysis:** Banking product ownership and credit card analysis.
5. **Geographical Analysis:** Customer distribution, churn rates, and balances across countries.
6. **Detailed View:** Customer-level records with churn and activity status.

## Key Business Insights

- Germany has the highest churn rate at **32.44%**.
- Customers aged **51–60** have a churn rate of **56.21%**.
- Female customers have a higher churn rate (**25.07%**) than male customers (**16.47%**).
- Inactive customers have a churn rate of **26.87%**, compared with **14.27%** for active customers.
- Customers holding three or four banking products show unusually high churn rates, although these segments are relatively small.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention analysis for customers in Germany.
- Investigate churn drivers among older customer segments.
- Develop customer engagement strategies for inactive members.
- Examine product-related churn patterns before making changes to banking product offerings.
- Monitor churn and retention KPIs regularly.
